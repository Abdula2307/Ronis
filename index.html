About Ronis

Ronis is a Pakistani tech accessories brand built around one idea: the things you use every day should be reliable. From fast GaN chargers that power up your phone and laptop in less time, to tough braided cables that don't fray, to smartwatches that track your health and keep up with your day, we bring modern, well-made tech at fair prices, delivered to your door.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Add the products you like to your cart, press Cart, enter your name, phone number, city and delivery address, and press Place order. That's it.

How do I pay?

We offer Cash on Delivery. You pay the delivery person when your order reaches you.

Do you deliver in my city?

Yes, we deliver across Pakistan. Just enter your city and full address at checkout.

How long does delivery take?

Orders usually arrive within 3 to 5 working days, depending on your city.

How will I know my order is confirmed?

We check every order and confirm it with you on the phone number you gave us, so please enter a number that is active.

Are the products original?

Yes. We sell quality-checked products and describe each one honestly on its page.

Can I cancel or change my order?

Yes, before it is confirmed and shipped. Contact us as soon as possible with your name and phone number.

What if I enter a wrong address or phone number?

Please double-check them before ordering. We can only deliver and confirm using the details you give us.
